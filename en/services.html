Cleaning & Staffing

Cleaning & Staffing - End-to-End Facility Management

A complete operational service system under one roof – from manpower, advanced cleaning technologies, and comprehensive facility management

ניקיון מקצועי

שירותי ניקיון מתקדמים לכל סוגי המבנים – משרדים, מסחר ותעשייה – באמצעות טכנולוגיות וציוד חדשני.

אספקת כוח אדם

גיוס, הכשרה וניהול עובדים מקצועיים לפרויקטים ומשימות תפעוליות בכל רחבי הארץ, בפריסה ארצית מלאה.

ניהול ואחזקת מבנים

ניהול תפעולי מלא של מבנים ומתחמים – אחזקה שוטפת, תיקונים, ופיקוח על כלל השירותים תחת קורת גג אחת.

טכנולוגיות מתקדמות ופתרונות מורכבים

Proces operates dedicated professional departments for complex work, using the most innovative technologies in the market. From rope access work (rappelling), through reverse osmosis window cleaning and nano coating, to flying a washing cannon using a drone – we bring precise solutions to every maintenance challenge.

Proces's certified rappelling teams perform cleaning, maintenance and sealing work at great heights while adhering to the strictest safety standards. An efficient and economical solution for high-rise buildings.

Unique service by Proces: flying a water or foam cannon using a thermal drone for washing facades, roofs and parking lots. Access to any point without need for scaffolding, with maximum speed and precision.

Our specialization department performs cleaning and disinfection of laboratories, clean rooms and factories using ozone gas (O3) technology and green ecological pest control, meeting the strictest international standards.

שירותי הליבה שלנו

ניקיון מבנים ומשרדים

Proces cleaning teams provide thorough cleaning services for offices, commercial buildings, and residential towers, using advanced equipment and proven work methods. We tailor the cleaning plan to the specific requirements of each client.

אספקת כוח אדם

Proces provides skilled manpower for the ongoing operations of businesses and organizations, from maintenance workers to logistics teams. We handle the recruitment, training, and placement of the most suitable employees, with a commitment to reliability and professionalism.

צוותי תמיכה לפרויקטים

For complex projects, we provide dedicated support teams deployed on-site at the client's location. The teams undergo specialized training and operate under the close management of Proces, with full adaptation to project requirements and timelines.

עבודות גובה מוסמכות

Proces operates certified high-altitude work teams for rappel window cleaning, facade maintenance, painting, and sealing at height, using advanced technologies including dedicated drones and an airborne water cannon for cleaning challenging facades.

שירותים לפי מגזר

מרכזי עסקים ומשרדים

Comprehensive daily cleaning, placement of permanent cleaning crews, ongoing maintenance management, high-rise window cleaning, floor scrubbing and coating, and stain removal of all types — all under one roof.

מגדלי מגורים

Common area cleaning, luxury lobby care, external window cleaning using rappelling and reverse osmosis, nano coating for facades, curtain replacement, and staffing of building maintenance teams.

מלונות ואירוח

Comprehensive cleaning of guest rooms, public areas, restaurants, and lobbies; carpet and upholstery cleaning, fragrance systems, ecological pest control, osmosis window cleaning, and professional staffing for housekeeping and maintenance departments.

חניונים ומגרשים

Thorough cleaning of underground and above-ground parking facilities, high-pressure floor washing, oil stain and graffiti removal, concrete scrubbing and coating, entrance plaza cleaning, and waste removal — keeping a clean and welcoming appearance.

מעבדות ומפעלים

Cleaning and disinfection of laboratories, clean rooms, and manufacturing plants, ozone gas disinfection, industrial carpet cleaning, and stubborn stain removal from all surfaces — to strict standards for sensitive environments.

למה פרוצס?

שגרת ניקיון יומית יציבה

A stable daily cleaning routine with dedicated teams permanently stationed on-site, ensuring continuity, in-depth familiarity with facility needs, and rapid response to any incident.

מחלקה טכנית מוסמכת

A specialized technical division for complex high-altitude work — rappelling, osmosis, nano coating — with close engineering supervision, compliance with international safety standards, and a proven track record of thousands of projects.

גמישות ומענה מדויק

A precise response to every need — from post-renovation cleaning to one-time supplementary services. We adapt the scope of service, schedules, and teams to your changing requirements.

צרו קשר עכשיו
Our Methodology

Proces Work Process

From area assessment to ongoing quality control – four simple steps that guarantee perfect results

Area Assessment and Workforce Matching

The project manager arrives on site, maps the unique needs of the facility, and matches the professional team or technical unit required – whether it's routine cleaning, complex height work or comprehensive management and maintenance services.

Quality Control and Ongoing Monitoring

After completion of work, rigorous quality control is performed. For contract clients we provide periodic reports and ongoing service array including surprise visits, satisfaction surveys, and dynamic team adjustment to the changing needs of the facility.

בקרת איכות קפדנית

לאחר סיום העבודה מתבצעת בקרת איכות יסודית ומקצועית בכל אתר ואתר, תוך הקפדה על הסטנדרטים הגבוהים ביותר.

דוחות תקופתיים ושקיפות מלאה

ללקוחות חוזה אנו מספקים דוחות תקופתיים מפורטים ומערך שירות מתמשך הכולל ביקורי פתע וסקרי שביעות רצון.

התאמה דינמית של הצוות לצרכים המשתנים של המתקן, תוך גמישות מלאה ומענה מהיר לכל דרישה.
